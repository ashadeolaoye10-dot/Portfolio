Traditional Fashion

Traditional Attire Showcase

Nigerian traditional attire represents identity, heritage, pride and creativity. Every outfit tells a unique cultural story.

Agbada

A flowing traditional outfit widely worn by Yoruba men during weddings, festivals and special occasions.

Aso-Oke

Handwoven fabric from Southwestern Nigeria, symbolizing prestige and cultural elegance.

Isi Agu

A respected ceremonial outfit worn by Igbo men during important cultural celebrations.

Babban Riga

A luxurious flowing robe commonly worn in Northern Nigeria during ceremonies and royal gatherings.

George Wrapper

A beautifully patterned fabric popular among the Niger Delta people for grand celebrations.

Ankara

A vibrant African print used to create modern and traditional outfits across Nigeria.
