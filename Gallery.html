Explore Nigeria

Discover Our Rich Cultures

Nigeria is home to hundreds of ethnic groups, each blessed with unique traditions, beautiful native attire and cultural heritage.

Yoruba

Famous for Agbada, Aso-Oke, Gele and colourful ceremonies.

Igbo

Known for Isi Agu attire, red caps and rich cultural festivals.

Hausa

Home of the elegant Babbar Riga and the colourful Durbar Festival.

Efik

Popular for beautifully embroidered wrappers and traditional beads.

Tiv

Recognized by the iconic black-and-white striped woven fabric.

Kalabari

Celebrated for George wrappers and elegant Niger Delta fashion.
